## Short answer
A trial converts to a paid subscription automatically unless the customer cancels before it ends.

## What happens
1. The customer is emailed three days before the trial ends
2. On the final day the saved payment method is charged
3. A successful charge starts the first paid period immediately
4. A failed charge suspends access and starts the retry sequence

## Edge cases
- No saved payment method: access stops at trial end and nothing is charged
- Cancelling during the trial ends access straight away rather than at the trial end date
