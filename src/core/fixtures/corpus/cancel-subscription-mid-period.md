---
title: Cancelling a subscription mid-period
keywords: cancel, cancels, cancelling, cancellation, subscription, mid-period, refund
---

## Short answer
Access continues until the end of the period the customer has already paid for, and no refund is issued.

## What happens
1. The subscription is flagged to stop at the end of the current period
2. The customer keeps full access until that date
3. No renewal charge is attempted when the date arrives

## Edge cases
- Still inside a trial: access ends immediately instead of at period end
- Payment already overdue: the subscription is cancelled at once
